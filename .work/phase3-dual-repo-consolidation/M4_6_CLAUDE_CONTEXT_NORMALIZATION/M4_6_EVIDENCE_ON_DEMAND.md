## What qualifies as historical here

Retrospective status/closure markers — headings literally containing
`[CLOSED, ...]`, "Audited Already Satisfied," "Confirmed Already
Satisfied," "Re-Verified, No Change," or narrative verification-pass
prose ("What is genuinely real...", "A real discrepancy this
verification pass found...", "What remains genuinely, honestly open").
These record *that a past audit happened and what it concluded*, not a
present-tense rule a new task must follow — the M4.6 spec's own S16
test ("historical/non-normative material should not remain ALWAYS_ON")
applies directly.
