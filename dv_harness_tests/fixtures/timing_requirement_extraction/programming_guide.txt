Section 4.2 Timing Requirements

The setup time for DATA relative to CLK shall be at least 5 ns (tSU >= 5 ns).
The hold time for DATA relative to CLK shall be at least 2 ns (tHOLD >= 2 ns).
Read latency shall not exceed 16 clock cycles.
Write latency must not exceed 20 ns.
